01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Builder by choice.

I feel stressed when I have a lot of things to do. When I’m stressed or in a bad mood, I play games because I’m a gamer. Playing with my friends, classmates, and friends from afar makes me feel at ease. In my daily life, I do household chores, prepare for school, and then play games. Sometimes my friends and I join a voice chat on Discord to play together and have fun. After that, I finish my assignments if I have any. Meeting my friends at ACLC gave me some of the best days I’ve ever had. Now, all of them have transferred to VSU in Baybay, and I’m the only one who has stayed at ACLC. I’ve met new classmates. Although I rarely talk to them, I’m gradually learning to get along with them and have fun together. Creating this portfolio has helped me realize who I am today.

My goal is to become a successful developer and create a game that people can enjoy. Every project is a chance to improve, solve problems, and turn an idea into something real.
